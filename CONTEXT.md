# Dawnload

Dawnload keeps downloads in a queue and can share completed torrents with their peers.

## Language

**Courtesy pause**. A seeding job temporarily stops serving peers because the computer is on battery or a metered network. It stays in Seeding and can continue when conditions change.

**Seed deadline**. The end of the time window that starts when the torrent first completes its file selection. Time keeps passing while Dawnload is closed or seeding is paused.

**Lifetime upload**. The total bytes Dawnload has uploaded for a torrent item across its runs. The seed ratio divides this total by the size of the currently selected files.
